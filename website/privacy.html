Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and your rights — including how to request data deletion.

Effective Date: 28 August 2026  |  Last Updated: 28 August 2026

1. Who We Are

WSE Lab Consult ("we," "us," "the Company") is an intelligent automation studio registered in Lagos, Nigeria. We build and operate SaaS products for African businesses. This Privacy Policy applies to all our websites, products, and services.

Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at info@wseailab.com.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account data: Name, email address, phone number, business name, password (hashed)
  • Payment data: Billing address and payment method details (processed and stored by our payment processors — we do not store full card numbers)
  • Communication data: Messages you send us via contact forms, email, or WhatsApp
  • Service data: Content and data you input into our SaaS products (e.g., audit records in WSE Audit System, customer data in SmartSales)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, timestamps, session duration
  • Device data: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution, IP address
  • Cookies: Essential cookies for authentication and session management (see Section 8)

2.3 Information from Third Parties

If you connect third-party services (e.g., Google Workspace, WhatsApp Business API), we may receive limited data as necessary to provide the integration.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on servers operated by our infrastructure providers (Vercel, Supabase, and Netlify), primarily located in the United States and European Union. We implement the following safeguards:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Role-based access controls with least-privilege principles
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Automated backups with point-in-time recovery
  • Password hashing using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: Payment processors, hosting providers, and email delivery services that process data on our behalf under binding agreements
  • Legal requirements: When required by Nigerian law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (you will be notified in advance)
  • With your consent: When you explicitly authorize sharing with a specific third party

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data is retained for the duration of your Subscription plus 30 days
  • Cancelled accounts: Account data is retained for 90 days to allow reactivation, then deleted
  • Financial records: Retained for 6 years as required by Nigerian tax law (FIRS)
  • Communication records: Retained for 2 years, then anonymized or deleted
  • Usage analytics: Aggregated and anonymized after 12 months

7. Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure (Deletion): Request deletion of your personal data (see Section 7.1)
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw marketing consent at any time

7.1 How to Request Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by following these steps:

  1. Send a request to info@wseailab.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address associated with your account, the specific product(s) involved, and a description of data to be deleted (or "all data")
  3. Identity verification: We will verify your identity within 3 business days by sending a confirmation code to your registered email
  4. Processing: Once verified, we will process your deletion request within 14 business days
  5. Confirmation: You will receive written confirmation when deletion is complete

Exceptions: We may retain certain data where required by law (e.g., financial records for tax compliance) or where necessary to resolve disputes. In such cases, we will inform you of the specific data retained and the reason.

You may also request deletion via WhatsApp at +234 816 082 9058. The same verification process applies.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication, session management, and security. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the Services to function.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Nigeria (specifically the United States and EU) through our infrastructure providers. These transfers are protected by:

  • Standard contractual clauses with our infrastructure providers
  • The provider's own data protection certifications and compliance programs
  • Technical safeguards (encryption, access controls) described in Section 4

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated via email and a prominent notice on our website at least 14 days before taking effect.

12. Complaints

If you believe your data rights have been violated, you may:

  1. Contact us directly at info@wseailab.com
  2. File a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng
