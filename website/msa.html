Master Service Agreement

The governing framework for consulting engagements, custom development projects, and enterprise SaaS arrangements with WSE Lab Consult.

Effective Date: 28 August 2026  |  Last Updated: 28 August 2026

1. Purpose

This Master Service Agreement ("MSA") establishes the general terms and conditions under which WSE Lab Consult ("Service Provider") provides consulting, custom software development, AI integration, and enterprise SaaS services to clients ("Client"). Individual engagements are governed by Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under this MSA.

2. Statements of Work

Each engagement will be defined by a SOW that specifies:

  • Scope of work and deliverables
  • Timeline and milestones
  • Fees, payment schedule, and currency
  • Acceptance criteria
  • Specific personnel or resources assigned
  • Any terms that supplement or modify this MSA for that engagement

In the event of conflict between this MSA and a SOW, the SOW prevails for that engagement.

3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Fee Structure

Fees are specified in each SOW and may be structured as:

  • Fixed price: A total fee for defined deliverables, billed per milestone
  • Time and materials: Billed at agreed hourly or daily rates
  • Monthly retainer: A fixed monthly fee for an agreed scope of ongoing services
  • Subscription: For enterprise SaaS access, billed monthly or annually

3.2 Payment Terms

  • Invoices are due within 14 days of issuance unless otherwise specified in the SOW
  • Payments may be made in Nigerian Naira (NGN) or US Dollars (USD) as specified
  • Late payments incur interest at 2% per month on the outstanding balance
  • WSE Lab Consult reserves the right to suspend work if payments are overdue by more than 30 days

3.3 Taxes

All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. The Client is responsible for any VAT, withholding tax, or other taxes arising from the engagement, except for WSE Lab Consult's income taxes.

4. Intellectual Property

4.1 Client Deliverables

Upon full payment, the Client owns all custom code, designs, and documentation created specifically for the Client under a SOW ("Client Deliverables"). WSE Lab Consult assigns all rights, title, and interest in Client Deliverables to the Client.

4.2 Pre-Existing IP

WSE Lab Consult retains ownership of all pre-existing intellectual property, frameworks, libraries, tools, and methodologies ("Pre-Existing IP"). Where Pre-Existing IP is incorporated into Client Deliverables, the Client receives a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use that Pre-Existing IP solely as part of the delivered solution.

4.3 General Knowledge

Nothing in this MSA prevents either party from using general knowledge, skills, and experience acquired during the engagement, provided that confidential information is not disclosed.

5. Confidentiality

Each party agrees to:

  • Keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party
  • Use confidential information solely for the purpose of performing under this MSA
  • Restrict access to confidential information to personnel who need to know
  • Maintain confidentiality obligations for 3 years after the termination of this MSA

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information that is publicly available, independently developed, lawfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law.

6. Warranties

WSE Lab Consult warrants that:

  • Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care, consistent with industry standards
  • Deliverables will materially conform to the specifications in the applicable SOW for 90 days after acceptance ("Warranty Period")
  • Deliverables will not knowingly infringe any third-party intellectual property rights

If a Deliverable does not meet the warranty, WSE Lab Consult will, at its option, repair or replace the non-conforming portion at no additional cost. This is the Client's sole remedy for warranty claims.

7. Limitation of Liability

  • Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages
  • WSE Lab Consult's total aggregate liability under this MSA shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Client under the applicable SOW in the 12 months preceding the claim
  • These limitations do not apply to breaches of confidentiality, intellectual property infringement, or willful misconduct

8. Indemnification

WSE Lab Consult will indemnify the Client against third-party claims that the Deliverables infringe intellectual property rights, provided that:

  • The Client promptly notifies WSE Lab Consult of the claim
  • WSE Lab Consult has sole control of the defense and settlement
  • The Client provides reasonable cooperation

The Client will indemnify WSE Lab Consult against claims arising from the Client's use of Deliverables in violation of this MSA or applicable law.

9. Term and Termination

9.1 Term

This MSA is effective from the date of execution and remains in effect until terminated. Individual SOWs have their own timelines.

9.2 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this MSA or any SOW with 30 days' written notice. The Client shall pay for all work completed through the termination date.

9.3 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party:

  • Materially breaches this MSA and fails to cure within 14 days of written notice
  • Becomes insolvent or enters bankruptcy proceedings

9.4 Effect of Termination

  • All outstanding invoices become immediately due
  • WSE Lab Consult will deliver all completed and in-progress work products
  • Each party will return or destroy the other party's confidential information
  • Sections on intellectual property, confidentiality, limitation of liability, and indemnification survive termination

10. Data Protection

Where the Services involve processing personal data, the terms of our Data Processing Agreement apply. Both parties will comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and other applicable data protection legislation.

11. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures caused by events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, government actions, pandemics, power outages, or internet disruptions. The affected party must notify the other party promptly and make reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact.

12. Independent Contractor

WSE Lab Consult is an independent contractor. Nothing in this MSA creates an employment, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship between the parties. WSE Lab Consult's personnel are not employees of the Client.

13. Non-Solicitation

During the term of this MSA and for 12 months after termination, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any personnel of the other party who were involved in the Services, without prior written consent.

14. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This MSA is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Disputes shall be resolved through:

  1. Negotiation: Good-faith discussion for 30 days
  2. Mediation: If negotiation fails, mediation at the Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse
  3. Arbitration: If mediation fails, binding arbitration in Lagos under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023

15. General Provisions

  • Entire agreement: This MSA, together with executed SOWs, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties for consulting and custom development services
  • Amendments: Changes to this MSA must be in writing and signed by both parties
  • Severability: If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in effect
  • Assignment: Neither party may assign this MSA without the other party's written consent, except in connection with a merger or acquisition
  • Notices: All notices must be in writing, sent to the contact information on the applicable SOW
