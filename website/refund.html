Refund Policy

Clear terms on refunds, cancellations, and billing for WSE Lab Consult's subscription products and consulting services.

Effective Date: 28 August 2026  |  Last Updated: 28 August 2026

1. SaaS Subscription Products

This section applies to all WSE Lab Consult subscription products, including Memora OS, WSE Datapilot, WSE Audit System, WSE SoundHub, Solwise Realty AI, SmartSales, WSE Academy, Wizzy AI, WSE Trading Bot, AI Lead Generator, WSE PostPilot, WSE AutoReply, and WhatsApp AutoReply.

1.1 Free Trials

Where offered, free trials provide full access to paid features for the trial period. No payment is charged during the trial. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your subscription will convert to a paid plan and you will be charged the applicable fee.

1.2 Monthly Subscriptions

  • Cancellation: You may cancel at any time. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No further charges will be made.
  • Refund window: If you cancel within 7 days of your initial purchase (first payment only), you may request a full refund.
  • After 7 days: Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable after the 7-day window. Your access continues until the end of the current billing cycle.

1.3 Annual Subscriptions

  • Refund window: You may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial annual purchase.
  • After 14 days: A prorated refund may be issued for the unused portion of the annual term, calculated from the date of the refund request, minus a 10% early termination fee.
  • Renewal refunds: Annual renewals may be refunded in full if requested within 7 days of the renewal charge.

1.4 Upgrades and Downgrades

  • Upgrades: When upgrading mid-cycle, you will be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing period. Upgrades are non-refundable.
  • Downgrades: When downgrading, the new lower rate takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. No refund is issued for the current cycle.

2. Consulting and Custom Development Services

This section applies to bespoke software development, automation consulting, and custom AI solutions provided under a Master Service Agreement or project-specific statement of work.

2.1 Milestone-Based Projects

  • Before work begins: If you cancel before any work has started, any advance payment will be refunded in full within 14 business days.
  • During the project: If you cancel mid-project, payment for completed milestones is non-refundable. Payment for incomplete milestones will be refunded, minus compensation for any work-in-progress (calculated at the hourly rate specified in the agreement).
  • Delivered milestones: Completed and accepted deliverables are non-refundable.

2.2 Retainer Agreements

  • Unused retainer hours do not roll over to the next month unless specified in the agreement.
  • Refunds for retainer agreements follow the cancellation terms in the specific agreement.

3. E-Commerce Products (WSE SoundHub)

For physical goods sold through WSE SoundHub:

  • Defective items: Full refund or replacement within 14 days of delivery upon verification of the defect
  • Change of mind: Returns accepted within 7 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. The customer bears return shipping costs.
  • Digital downloads: Non-refundable once downloaded, except for technical issues that prevent use

4. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email: Send your request to info@wseailab.com with the subject line "Refund Request"
  2. Include: Your full name, the email associated with your account, the product or service, the date of purchase, and the reason for the refund
  3. Processing time: Refund requests are reviewed within 5 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 10 business days.
  4. Payment method: Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Bank transfers may take an additional 3-5 business days to reflect.

You may also initiate a refund request via WhatsApp at +234 816 082 9058.

5. Non-Refundable Items

The following are non-refundable:

  • Setup fees and one-time configuration charges
  • Domain name registrations
  • Third-party service fees passed through to you (e.g., SMS credits, API usage)
  • Accounts terminated for violation of our Terms of Service

6. Chargebacks

We encourage you to contact us before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. Filing a chargeback without first attempting resolution with us may result in account suspension and forfeiture of any refund eligibility. We will cooperate with payment providers to resolve disputes fairly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes will be communicated via email to active subscribers.
